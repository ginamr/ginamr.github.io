Baily's Beads

@UCI

At UCI I created a 2D video game named Baily’s Beads.

I created gaming assets which include: a ufo sprite with flashing lights, the starry night sky game play background, the company iogames logo, the game logo, an alien, an astronaut, a sprite of the sun which appears to expand and contract.

  • My team consisted of 5 additional members and we utilized agile principals to manage the project.
  • We organized our time into 3 sprints which each lasted 2 weeks.
  • We created our own scrum and backlog boards using Google Docs.
  • We utilized scrum boards to assign tasks, estimate deliverable timelines, and report status.
  • We synced three times a week for an hour or so to hash out ideas and come up with solutions to existing problems.
  • We had white board discussions, created wire frame designs, and maintained documentation.
